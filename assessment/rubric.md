# Scoring rubric

Each of the six dimensions is scored 0–4. The anchors below are deliberately written so two reviewers should agree within one point.

| Score | Label | Anchor |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | Absent | No evidence the dimension has been considered. |
| 1 | Ad hoc | Considered informally; no documentation; depends on specific individuals. |
| 2 | Defined | Documented in at least one place; inconsistent application across the team. |
| 3 | Managed | Consistently applied; owned by a named role; reviewed on a cadence. |
| 4 | Optimized | Measured, instrumented, and improving against a baseline. |

## Aggregation

- **Total score** is the simple sum (0–24).
- **No dimension below 2** is required to recommend *go*. A single 0 or 1 forces *fix-then-go* regardless of total.
- **Governance (dimension 5) below 2** is a hard *no-go* for any AI workload touching regulated data.

## Evidence requirement

Every score must cite at least one of:

- A document (link, file path, or document control ID).
- A system-of-record screenshot or export.
- A named interview with date and role (not name) of interviewee.

Scores without evidence are reported as **U** (unsupported) and called out in the report. Unsupported scores do not contribute to the recommendation.
